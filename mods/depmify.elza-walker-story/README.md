# Elza Walker - Jill Story Skin

Mod author: Depmify
Version: 1.0.0

Adds Elza Walker from Resident Evil 1.5 as an additional Jill appearance in
Story Mode's PLEASE SELECT MAIN CAST screen. Select Jill and use the up/down
skin controls to choose Elza Walker. The original Jill appearances remain
available. This package adds one Story skin and does not add a Tyrant Battle
character or globally replace Jill's model.

Elza keeps Jill's campaign behavior, animations, starting inventory, damage,
grab, death and dodge sounds. The supplied preview is also used as her portrait.
The original PL04.PLD model, including its ponytail data, is included unchanged.
SHATTERED handles its model adaptation.

## Installation

Download from MODS > BROWSE, restart SHATTERED, then enable the mod in MODS and
choose Apply. For a manual installation, place this ZIP in the Mods folder
beside SHATTERED.exe; do not extract it there. Restart, enable it and Apply.
No other mods are required.

In online or LAN Story sessions, other players can receive the skin temporarily
when ALLOW OTHER USERS CONTENT permits it. Players who disable that option need
the matching skin installed and enabled locally.
